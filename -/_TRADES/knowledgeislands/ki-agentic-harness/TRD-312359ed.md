# TRD-312359ed: Distinguish rubric audit evidence from remediation authority

## Context

The tools-ki CLI delivery surfaced reusable governance learnings around command grammar, completions, manual alignment, and the difference between machine-detectable gaps and repairs requiring human judgment. The ki-tools rubric currently classifies audit evidence as mechanical or judgmental, but remediation authority is implicit in whether a mechanical item happens to expose a conform callback.

## Submission

Evolve the rubric contract and ki-tools catalogue to state two independent dimensions for every criterion: audit evidence (mechanical, heuristic, or judgmental) and remediation authority (automatic, guided human action, or report-only). Render both dimensions in the generated rubric and ki repo educate output. Preserve the host rule that only bounded safe proposals are automatically published. Promote reusable CLI/manual lessons to appropriately mechanical or hybrid checks: one canonical completion action, no retired command aliases, aligned help/README/changelog/manual surfaces, and separately documented command entries rather than pipe-joined subcommands.

## Constraints

ki-agentic-harness owns the rubric contract and ki-tools capability. Do not write tools-ki as part of this trade. Judgment must never trigger an automatic mutation; guided-human remediation is documentation and review guidance, not host publication. Keep tool-specific policies out of ki-tools: trade vocabulary belongs to ki-trades, coverage and source modularity to ki-engineering, and ki command ordering to tools-ki. Maintain current safe transaction and post-conform re-audit semantics.
