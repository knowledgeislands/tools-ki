# TRD-27279159: Adopt mutable pre-trade phases

## Context

The documentation-governance submission [TRD-6f63fb71](TRD-6f63fb71.md) revealed a practical limitation in the current trade contract: useful clarification can emerge after a sender has created its immutable outbound record but before the receiver has reviewed it. The sender needs a local place to develop, refine, and link the emerging knowledge without rewriting a sent submission or mutating receiver-owned state.

## Submission

Extend `ki-trades` with sender-local, mutable pre-trade items. A pre-trade may capture evolving context, phased preparation, roadmap links, and related draft knowledge entirely within the sender repository. Finalisation validates the declared route and canonical envelope, freezes the sender payload, and creates the sent record. Only that final sent record becomes eligible for receiver intake and the existing receiver-owned decision lifecycle. The pre-trade lifecycle should let a sender consolidate clarification such as the explicit ownership of `docs/features/` by `ki-feature-definitions` before submission rather than emitting a succession of near-duplicate sent records.

## Constraints

The sender may write and revise pre-trade material only in its own repository. It must not create or alter a receiver copy before finalisation, infer a receiver decision, or treat local drafting as delivery. Sent payloads remain immutable under the current sender/receiver authority model. The Harness retains all decisions on phases, commands, storage layout, identifiers, validation, and migration of existing records.
