# TRD-094f7987: Review public contract coverage

## Context

The completed tools-ki Feature Definitions corpus covered every top-level command family but still omitted several tested public subcommands until an explicit review compared the corpus with the CLI grammar and contract tests.

## Submission

Add judgment guidance that a Feature Definitions review compares a product's public contract inventory with its corpus and records every surface as a requirement, a Gap, or an explicit area-level exclusion. Do not require one requirement per command or make semantic coverage a synthetic mechanical finding.

## Constraints

Keep the rule product-agnostic, preserve bounded areas and requirement focus, and leave the inventory source and adequacy of each coverage decision to human review. The Harness retains all decisions on standard wording and rubric placement.
