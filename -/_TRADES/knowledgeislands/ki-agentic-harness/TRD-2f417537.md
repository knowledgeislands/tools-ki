# TRD-2f417537: Model durable trade delivery

## Context

A sender may finalise a trade and then disconnect before the receiver has observed, received, reviewed, or acted on it. A durable trade therefore cannot depend on live sender availability after finalisation. Local roadmap work may also wait at materially different points: until receipt is observed, until the receiver makes a decision, or until the response and any receiver follow-on work establish what local work can proceed.

## Submission

Define delivery and waiting as separate observable trade stages. A sender-local pre-trade is mutable; finalisation creates a self-contained immutable sent record that survives sender disconnection. The receiver independently receives that record when it can, then owns its review and decision lifecycle. Extend the roadmap-trade integration so a `waiting-for` item names both a trade and the exact observed condition it needs: receipt, a receiver decision, or an explicitly linked receiver follow-on record. The model must distinguish waiting for delivery from waiting for processing or a response that determines local work.

## Constraints

Do not make sender availability, a live connection, or remote mutation a precondition for receiver processing. Do not infer that a receiver's `in_progress` status means its follow-on work is complete. Preserve receiver authority over its copy, decision, priority, and follow-on records; the sender may only observe the published state. The Harness retains all decisions on stage names, schema, polling or discovery behaviour, command grammar, and migration of existing sent records.
