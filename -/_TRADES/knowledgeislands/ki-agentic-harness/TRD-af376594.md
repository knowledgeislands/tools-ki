# TRD-af376594: Formalise repository-local KI self governance

## Context

`tools-ki` is establishing its committed `.agents/skills/ki-self/` source as repository-local governance, rather than treating it as another form of `AGENTS.md`. Its initial rubric covers the host's bootstrap inventory, managed-skill classification, and repair coverage. The harness already recognises repository-authored `ki-self` sources and derived runtime links, but does not yet define their portable skill or native-rubric contract.

## Submission

Formalise `ki-self` as repository-local governance: define its boundary from always-loaded instructions and portable harness skills; specify its required shape, rubric publication and native audit/conform lifecycle; and establish how a local source is discovered and projected without becoming an installed harness capability. Define the auditable relationship between authoritative classified inventories, activation, and repair for hosts that manage skills or other derived state.

## Constraints

The receiving repository retains all decisions on portable semantics and rollout. Preserve the current authority boundaries: the repository authors and commits its source; runtime projections are derived managed links; and the `ki` host controls execution and writes. Do not make repository-local `ki-self` a bootstrap user skill or require every repository to carry one. Keep the standard runtime-neutral and avoid a compatibility fallback or repository-vendored executor.
