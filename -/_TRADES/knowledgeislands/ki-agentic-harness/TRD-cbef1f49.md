# TRD-cbef1f49: Assess recap coverage matrix

## Context

A prior cross-repository recap presented a compact matrix of discussion points, their owning home, and their disposition. It gave the user a reliable way to check that every topic had been captured in a roadmap item or otherwise resolved.

## Submission

Assess whether ki-recap should offer a concise discussion-coverage matrix: each material discussion point, its owning repository or artifact, and a state such as captured, delivered, pending, or decision-needed. Define when the matrix is appropriate, how it stays evidence-backed, and whether a mechanical audit can check its shape while leaving completeness judgmental.

## Constraints

Keep recap grounded in available transcript and repository evidence; do not claim exhaustive coverage where evidence is incomplete. Preserve concise human-readable output and keep any future rubric distinction explicit between mechanically auditable structure and judgmental completeness.
