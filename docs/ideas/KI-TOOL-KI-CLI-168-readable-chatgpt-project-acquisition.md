## Discussion

### Coverage and fidelity

Project enumeration must establish its actual coverage rather than treating a bounded account export or a list of titles as complete membership. Preserve complete conversations and write-ups, required original assets and their provenance; omissions or unreadable content must remain visible. Opaque local-store records are change evidence, not readable conversation content. A digest or preview cannot substitute for the promised complete material.

### Source decision

The first planning decision is which authorised readable source satisfies enumeration and completeness after the Harness evaluation. Keep unsupported coverage claims unresolved; this intake chooses no provider route, credential or private API. The acquisition outcome is distinct from failure reporting, retirement design and proof of a complete move.
