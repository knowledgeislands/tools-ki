# TRD-3f6649a4: Validate pruned batch outcomes

## Context

Closed batch records become invalid after their accepted roadmap items are legitimately pruned because tools-ki resolves every item only from the current working tree. Harness retention policy permits committed-history outcome evidence.

## Submission

Add closed-batch archival validation against the exact close-evidence commit, strengthen close to prove every item exists there at the requested target state, retain live canonical validation for open batches, and cover roadmap plus KB Streams without restoration or mutation.

## Constraints

tools-ki owns CLI mechanics; Harness semantics remain unchanged. Closed validation must be read-only, network-free, dependency-order aware, valid after item pruning, and must not weaken expiry gates for open run or close operations.
